## What the map says

1. **The attack surface is real.** Prediction preserved at 0.99 across every cell, so prediction-level monitoring is blind to all of it.
2. **Displacement attacks are severe and statistically undetectable.** A tiny, prediction-preserving perturbation replaces 49%–68% of the shown top-5 on every dataset (top-k Jaccard distance 0.66–0.81), at best-signal AUROC 0.58–0.67 — chance. No signal here sees it.
3. **Scaffolding is the one detectable attack** (best-signal AUROC up to 0.78, but see Tab5 — cross-method consensus alone clears 0.5 on only two of the three datasets), and only because it fools *perturbation-based* explainers. An exact explainer is uncorrupted (corrupt 0.00–0.06), which is architectural: the scaffold watches for the off-manifold queries a perturbation-based explainer must make, and an exact attributor never makes them. Guidance: prefer an exact attributor where the model admits one. This rests on the query mechanism, not on any claim about LIME's attributions being unreliable.
4. **Misdirection is weak in the current search** (corrupt 0.17–0.59) and also undetectable. Strengthening it is open work.
5. **The conformal false-alarm guarantee.** Pooled false-alarm rate 0.056 against α=0.05 — ABOVE NOMINAL, investigate before claiming anything. Individual cells range 0.00–0.20; the guarantee is on the rate in expectation, not on every cell of a 15-run sample, and Fig6 tests it properly with exact binomial intervals over pooled held-out flows.

## Where the negative results live

The certified-stability signal fails here, and our first account of why was wrong twice over. It is not that clean attributions are unstable (2%-21% of them certify), and it is not simply that the certificate saturates — saturation is a property of the tree pipeline, and a gradient pipeline on the same data does not saturate and still fails. What rules the signal out is that its AUROC ranges from 0.09 to 0.84 across datasets, detector/attributor pairs and seeds, sometimes pointing backwards, with no label-free way to know which regime you are in. The claim is *not deployable*, never *broken*.

Both corrections are measured; `scripts/summarize_signal1.py` and `scripts/run_generality.py` regenerate them.
